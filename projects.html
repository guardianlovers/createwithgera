01 / 07 — Product

OurSpace

Problem

Filipino fiction writers were juggling five or more separate apps — docs for drafting, spreadsheets for outlines, chat apps for character notes, socials for reader feedback — with nothing built for how they actually write.

Approach

Designed and built a single writing studio combining drafting, chapter organization, outline tools, and community feedback into one connected flow, launched in early access.

Outcome

A private studio that replaced five scattered apps with one.

02 / 07 — Nonprofit

Síkat-Aurora Inc.

Problem

A youth-led literacy nonprofit in Baler, Aurora needed a site that could turn visitors into volunteers, donors, and sponsors — not just explain the mission.

Approach

Structured the entire site around three clear action paths — volunteer, donate, sponsor — so every visitor has an obvious next step regardless of how they want to help.

Outcome

A youth-led movement site organized for action, not just awareness.

03 / 07 — Landing page

Learn with Teacher Aiah

Problem

Approach

Rebuilt the landing page around a single, focused booking action, with pricing and trial details front and center instead of buried in navigation.

Outcome

A tutor landing page built around one clear inquiry action.

Problem

Approach
