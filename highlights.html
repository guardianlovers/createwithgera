Mar 2026 — PresentFounder

Founding OurSpace

Product Designer · OurSpace Writing Platform

In March 2026 I founded OurSpace, a productivity platform built for Filipino fiction writers. I own the product strategy, the UX flows, and how community feedback shapes the roadmap. It reached over 1,000 users within its first month — proof that writers needed a private space to build a habit in, not another platform built around publishing and performance.

Sep 2024 — PresentFull-time

Designing at scale with Abenson

UI/UX Designer · Abenson Ventures Inc.

Since September 2024 I've worked end-to-end on UX/UI for one of the Philippines' major retailers, across retail, e-commerce, and PWA projects. A highlight has been leading design initiatives like the Google Review Automation dashboard and several PWA redesigns, alongside ongoing work to make customer-facing pages and product discovery easier to navigate.
